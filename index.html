Xuan Luo罗璇

Research Scientist, Google · Project Beam

I am a Research Scientist at Google, where I work on novel view synthesis for the Project Beam team. My research interests include long-context modeling, efficient transformers, 3D vision, generative models, and computational photography.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Washington, where I was advised by Steven M. Seitz, Jason Lawrence, and Ricardo Martin-Brualla, and interned at Google, Disney Research Zurich, and Facebook. I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with Hongtao Lu, and I was a visiting student at the National University of Singapore, collaborating with Shuicheng Yan.
